This will be an astrology web app, written in react.js, that users will leverage to create a list of conjunctions. These will all be conjunctions of the moon or the sun with the position of astrological objects from their natal chart. The web application will output a list of the conjunctions to the user. There is no authentication required, no user database. People will just enter their date and time of birth, location of birth, and the app will do the rest.
